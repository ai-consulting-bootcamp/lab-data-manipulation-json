I created FamilySize (SibSp + Parch + 1), IsAlone, and AgeGroup, retaining an Unknown group for missing ages. These features reveal descriptive survival differences: accompanied passengers survived at 50.6% versus 30.4% for those travelling alone, while family-size and age-group tables show additional variation. Using Passenger and TitanicDataset classes taught me to organize records, reuse summary and export logic, and consistently convert missing values and numeric types into valid JSON.
